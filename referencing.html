HKDSE Paper 1 Reference Rules & Taxonomy

Core referral categories tested in HKDSE English Language Paper 1.

Referent Category Target Words in Text How to Identify the Referent
Subject Pronouns I, you, he, she, it, we, they Match person & gender. She = singular female; they = plural noun or group of people.
Object Pronouns me, you, him, her, it, us, them Receives the action. them refers back to a plural noun mentioned earlier.
Demonstrative Pronouns this, that, these, those this/that = singular idea or noun; these/those = plural items or facts.
Indefinite Pronouns & Numbers all, both, few, many, several, 20, 40 Refers to a specific quantity of previously mentioned items (e.g., 40 friendship bracelets $\rightarrow$ all of these).
Adverbs of Place & Time here, there, now, then there points to a location (e.g. Cambodia); then points to a specified time/year (e.g. in 2010).
Determiner + Noun Phrase the iconic sitcom, such challenges Combines determiner (the, such, this) + noun. Summarizes an entity (e.g. Friends $\rightarrow$ The iconic sitcom).
Whole Idea / Clause Referents that, this phenomenon, such young people Refers to an entire clause or situation described in 1-2 preceding sentences rather than a single word.

Golden Exam Tip for HKDSE Students

Always perform the Substitution Test! Take your candidate referent phrase and plug it directly into the sentence in place of the pronoun or reference word. If the sentence becomes grammatically awkward or logically redundant, keep scanning backward!
